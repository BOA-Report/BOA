What SEO is doing Our GMB profiles are generating consistent, high-intent call volume. 55.9% of April's recorded HCP revenue is traceable to SEO-originated leads. The SEO-sourced average job value ($2,068) outperforms the overall HCP April average ($1,204) by 71%. The channels are working and driving quality leads.
The revenue gap — the full picture The shortfall Daniel is experiencing is a combination of two factors: a seasonal pattern that repeats every year (April–May are historically soft months, visible clearly in both 2024 and 2025 data), and a meaningful number of leads that came in through SEO channels but did not make it into HCP as closed jobs. Improving follow-up tracking and capturing disposition on unclosed leads would help quantify where revenue is being lost and allow us to address it systematically.
2026 vs. 2025 — the data tells a different story than the feeling April 2026 ($51,796) outperformed April 2025 ($47,002) by +10.2%. May 2026 ($46,322) outperformed May 2025 ($32,657) by +41.8%. January 2026 ($106,422) was the strongest January in three years of HCP data. SEO engagement coincides with a measurable uplift in revenue during the months it has been active.
